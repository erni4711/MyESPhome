## Why HATi?

**ESPHome:** ESPHome allows me to integrate microcontrollers efficiently into my Home Assistant environment and implement complex automations easily. However, its options for controlling and configuring displays are relatively limited because the display layouts are static.

**HomeTiles:** HomeTiles allows me to design microcontroller displays flexibly and dynamically. Unlike ESPHome, it lets me customize display content and add interactive elements, significantly improving the usability and functionality of my projects. However, I find it inconvenient that an additional MQTT proxy is required to communicate with sensors and other information from Home Assistant.

**That is why HATi:** HATi combines the advantages of ESPHome and HomeTiles by bringing together ESPHome's simple integration and automation with HomeTiles' flexible and dynamic display design. This allows me to integrate microcontrollers efficiently into my Home Assistant environment while customizing the displays without relying on an additional MQTT proxy.
